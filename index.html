Weekly experiment readout · Week 38

Pricing ships, two tests need more time, one is blocked

Consumer subscription app (name withheld)Prepared by Jay Wadhwani, VP of ProductSources: PostHog, Mixpanel, ChartMogul, billing

Portfolio sample. This is a composite of real weekly readouts I wrote. The company name, people, feature names, and flag IDs are removed, and every figure has been changed. The structure, the statistical calls, and the decisions are real.

The short version

  1. Ship tiered pricing to 90% of traffic and keep a 10% holdout. Revenue per plans-page viewer is 3.9x control. Trial-to-paid is 2.7 points lower, inside our 3-point guardrail and within noise.
  2. Nothing else is ready to call. The recommendations test is flat so far and underpowered for the 2-point effect we planned for. Passwordless sign-up is 4 days old.
  3. The landing-page test has not started. Paid traffic never arrived, and the UTM tags don't match the test arms. Hold ad spend until the tags are fixed.
  4. Top-of-funnel risk: signup-to-trial is 13%, about half of May. It started falling before any of this week's tests and is the top item on the backlog.

Next: Ramp to 90% with a 10% control on the new signup flow. The holdout costs about $10K a month and is the only way to separate a pricing effect from a funnel effect. A plan-selection fix ships next week to close the LTV gap. Go/no-go on 100% in 14 days.

Next: At ~115 visitors per arm per day, the planned 2-week window can only detect a 3-point effect. Detecting 2 points takes about 30 days. Recommendation: stop the teaser arm now, because 87% of people never used it, and run the redesign for 30 days against control.

Data quality checks

  • Billing-derived trial events arrive in analytics 1–2 days late and backfill. Recent days are understated for every arm by the same amount. Comparisons stay fair, but absolute rates will rise.
  • Traffic split is healthy (chi-square p ≈ 0.16). One arm runs slightly light in both tools, which points to assignment, not tracking. Watching it.
  • Internal test accounts are still pinned to one arm of the recommendations test. They haven't entered the sample yet; removing them this week.

Decisions needed

  1. Approve the 90 / 10 pricing ramp starting Monday.
  2. Recommendations test: extend to 30 days to detect a 2-point effect, or accept a 3-point bar and call it on Sep 30. I recommend extending.
  3. Hold paid landing-page spend until tagging is verified.

Added to the learnings library

Tiered pricing

Hypothesis
Longer plans lift revenue per viewer without hurting conversion.
Result
3.9x first-payment revenue per viewer; conversion within guardrail.
Takeaway
Our users pay for a season, not a month. Track LTV per viewer, not just take rate, since plan choice can hide a drag.

Feature teaser at the trial wall

Hypothesis
A hands-on teaser pulls visitors into a trial.
Result
87% never engaged with it; primary metric flat.
Takeaway
When the mechanism doesn't fire, stop the arm. More sample won't fix low engagement.
